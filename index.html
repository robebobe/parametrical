One rail and eight meanings

The construction drawing has a rail numbered 1 to 26, one stop per letter, with a circle at each end. Around it sit eight anchors, each one a property a letter can have. Tap an anchor or a stop on the rail to see which letters it belongs to.

The rule

Two triangles that meet at the letter's number

The pivot, the one sharp corner of every glyph, sits on the rail at the letter's position in the alphabet. From there, two triangles reach out to the anchors that describe it. Every anchor is a circle with a 23-unit radius, so all the other corners are rounded.

Triangle 1 · the numberLeft end of the rail → odd or even, plus the structure anchor if it is angular or mixed.
Triangle 2 · the letter typeRight end of the rail → vowel or consonant, plus the structure anchor if it is round or orthogonal.

Angular and mixed sit above the rail, with the number anchors, so they join triangle 1. Round and orthogonal sit below it, with vowel and consonant, so they join triangle 2.

Placementrail at y 182 or y 314

The drawing sits at two heights

Inside the 800-unit letter cell, the whole structure drawing is placed at one of two heights, set by the structure type. For angular and mixed letters the rail sits at y 182 and their structure anchor lands at y 605. For round and orthogonal letters the drawing moves up 132 units, so the rail sits at y 314 and their structure anchor lands on the baseline.

Shared codes9 codes · 26 letters

Same code, different number

Only nine combinations of structure, parity and letter type occur. Letters with the same combination use exactly the same anchors, so the only thing that tells them apart is where the pivot sits on the rail. Each drawing overlays every letter in its group, with each pivot marked by its letter.

How it was made

From a classification to a font file

  1. Classify the alphabet

    Each of the 26 letters gets four properties: its number, whether that number is odd or even, whether it is a vowel or a consonant, and one of four structure types: angular, mixed, round or orthogonal.

  2. Draw the structure

    In Illustrator, the properties become places: a rail of 26 stops between two circles, and eight anchors around it. Numbers are cyan, letter types green, structures red.

  3. Connect each letter

    The pivot goes on the letter's stop. Triangle 1 joins it to the left end and the odd or even anchor; triangle 2 joins it to the right end and the vowel or consonant anchor. The structure anchor joins whichever triangle is on its side of the rail.

  4. Set it as a font

    The shapes were drawn in Glyphs 4.1.1 on a 1000-unit em, every letter 800 units wide, and exported as Parametrical-Regular.otf with CFF outlines. The whole font is 3,320 bytes.
